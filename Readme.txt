# Tailwind Sales & Profitability Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project features an interactive, two-page Power BI dashboard developed as part of the Microsoft Power BI Data Analyst Professional Certificate Capstone Project on Coursera.

The objective was to prepare and transform business data, develop a structured data model, create DAX measures, and build interactive reports for analyzing sales performance, revenue trends, product performance, customer loyalty points, and profitability.

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Preparation — Microsoft Excel

* Reviewed and organized the source data.
* Performed initial data cleaning and preparation.
* Prepared the dataset for further transformation and analysis in Power BI.

### 2. Data Transformation & Profiling — Power Query

* Imported the source data into Power BI.
* Examined column quality, column distribution, and data profiling information.
* Applied the required data transformations.
* Reviewed data consistency before loading the prepared data.

### 3. Data Modeling

* Developed a data model to support business analysis and reporting.
* Created an additional country table for country-level analysis.
* Created a USD sales table to support sales analysis in US dollars.
* Prepared the model to support measures and visualizations across relevant business dimensions.

### 4. DAX Measures

Created measures for business and financial performance reporting, including:

* Year-to-date (YTD) profit
* Net profit
* Profit margin and overall margin
* Sales and revenue-related metrics

### 5. Dashboard Development

#### Page 1: Sales Overview

Focuses on sales performance and related business metrics, including:

* Sales and quantity-related KPIs
* Median sales trends over time
* Loyalty points by country
* Quantity sold by product
* Interactive country filtering
* Additional visualizations for exploring sales distribution

#### Page 2: Profit Overview

Focuses on revenue and profitability, including:

* Gross revenue
* Year-to-date profit
* Net revenue in USD
* Profit margin trends by year, quarter, month, and day
* Net revenue by product
* Profit margin comparisons by country

## Business Analysis Focus

The dashboard enables users to explore:

* Sales performance across products and countries
* Changes in sales and profitability over time
* Product-level revenue contribution
* Differences in profit margins across countries
* Key financial performance indicators

These analytical views can support further investigation of business performance and potential improvement opportunities.
